# LOB routing guide

These rules override general insurance meaning.

## Routing rules

1. Who owns the policy decides the LOB. If the policy is a group scheme or is owned
   or paid for by a company (keyman, buy-sell, staff benefits), route to Corporate
   Insurance, even for servicing-style actions like adding members, changing the
   billing contact, or submitting a claim.
2. Claims on individually owned policies are handled by Policy Servicing.
3. Anything touching Vitality goes to AIA Vitality, including Vitality membership
   fee billing, Vitality premium discounts on renewal, and wearable or app data
   not syncing into points.
4. Any login, OTP, password, 2FA, or system error, for agents or for customers,
   goes to IT Helpdesk, regardless of which product the customer holds.

## Glossary

- iPoS: the agent point-of-sale app for illustrations and e-submissions.
- GIRO: bank direct debit for premium payments.
- ILP: investment-linked policy.
- Agent portal: the web portal agents use for policy enquiries and commission statements.
